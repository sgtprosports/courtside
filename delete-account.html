CourtSide Account & Data Deletion

We respect your privacy. If you wish to delete your CourtSide account and associated personal data, you can request permanent deletion by following these steps:

  1. Send an email from your registered account email to support@sgtprosports.com with the subject line: "Account Deletion Request - CourtSide".
  2. Include your username and registered email address in the message body.

Data Retained vs. Deleted: Upon confirmation, your user profile, custom saved court favorites, and personal check-in history will be permanently deleted from our databases within 7 business days.
