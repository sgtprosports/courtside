Privacy Policy for Courtside

Last updated: October 5, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Courtside collects location data (GPS) solely to help users find nearby basketball courts, calculate distances, and add new courts. We also store user-submitted ratings, reviews, photos, videos, and favorites securely.

2. How We Use Information

The information collected is used exclusively to provide, maintain, and improve the Courtside app functionality, including displaying crowd levels and court community feedback.

3. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@sgtprosports.com.
